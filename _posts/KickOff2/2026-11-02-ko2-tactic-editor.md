**Kick Off 2** on Amiga was a source of great fun for many people. Player Manager's tactic editor added a great deal of depth, turning matches into a balanced mix of skill and strategy.

With the help of Claude Code, we built a tool to create new tactics and save them directly into Amiga disk files. Have fun, and feel young again!

The **Kick Off 2 Tactic Editor** is a free Windows program. You move the players on a pitch for each ball zone and special situation, then
write your tactics to an Amiga disk image to use in the game.

**[Download it from GitHub](https://github.com/4phycs/Release_KO2_Tactic_Editor/releases/latest)**

![The tactic editor]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/ko2-tactic-editor/editor.png)

### What it does

- Edit the positions of the 10 outfield players in all 20 situations: 12 ball zones, kick-offs, goal kicks and corners.
- Study or clone the 8 standard tactics, which are included.
- Keep a tactic within a chosen distance of a standard tactic, with the areas outside the limits greyed out.
- Draw a second tactic over yours, and watch the players move between situations.
- Import tactics from Amiga disk images (`.adf` / `.sdf`) and export up to 20 to a new tactic disk.
- Browse teams and skills from your own Kick Off 2 or Player Manager game disk, or from Player Manager saved careers.

![The Import / Export page]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/ko2-tactic-editor/import_export.png)

### How to run it

Download the zip, unzip the whole folder, double-click `KO2TacticEditor.exe` and open <http://localhost:5000> in your browser.
The exe isn't digitally signed, so Windows may warn you the first time. If so, click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.

Instructions, the changelog and the license are on the **[GitHub page](https://github.com/4phycs/Release_KO2_Tactic_Editor)**.
